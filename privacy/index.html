Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

About this policy

This policy describes the current Waha:Zehib iOS app (com.zehib.app). Waha:Zehib lets you keep a coffee profile, select or capture a cup photo, write tasting notes, explore photo styles and interact with coffee-themed content. The current version stores your activity on your device; it does not connect profiles, invitations, posts or messages to a community server.

Information stored on your device

The app stores your chosen profile name, coffee preferences, entry status, likes, invitation text and dates, accepted conversations, messages, notification preferences, draft notes, draft category and a selected or captured draft image. These records support the corresponding screens and are stored in the app’s private files and system preferences. The app includes prepared coffee content and conversations; these are not evidence of communication with another person.

Camera, photos and speech

Camera access is used to capture a coffee photo. Photos access is requested when choosing a photo; the selected image can be copied into the app as a draft. Microphone and speech recognition access are requested for dictating tasting notes. Audio is processed on-device when supported; otherwise Apple speech recognition services may receive audio for transcription. Waha:Zehib does not save an audio recording. Dictated text becomes part of your notes when you save it. You can decline permissions or change them in iOS Settings.

Notifications

The app can request notification permission through iOS. The current version does not register with a remote push delivery service. Declining notification permission does not prevent you from using the local coffee features.

Network services and third parties

The app does not include an advertising or analytics integration and does not sell personal information. Coffee photo effects run on-device. The bundled Rive animation is loaded locally without CDN loading. Opening these legal pages makes an HTTPS request to the website host, which necessarily receives connection information such as your IP address and browser request details. These pages contain no analytics scripts, advertising or embedded tracking resources. Apple services operate under Apple’s own privacy terms.

Purchases

The Aroma Shop currently displays a catalog only. Purchase buttons do not initiate payment, and the displayed balance is not a record of purchased value. Waha:Zehib does not receive payment card details. If purchasing is enabled in a future release, this policy will be updated to describe transaction processing and retention.

Retention and deletion

Local records remain until you replace them, clear the app’s data or remove the app. In Me → Preferences & Privacy → Clear All Data, you can remove your saved profile, preferences, likes, invitations, conversations, messages and draft photo from this installation. This also clears pending and delivered app notifications. Original photos in your Photos library are unaffected. Device backups managed by Apple may retain app data according to your backup settings; Waha:Zehib does not provide its own cloud synchronization or recovery service.

Your choices and changes

Use iOS Settings to manage permissions and device backups. Avoid entering sensitive information into notes or messages. This policy will be revised if the app’s data handling changes; the revision date appears above. Privacy questions and requests may be directed to the contact address below.
